## 5.1CH Electronic Volume with 4 Input Selector

- REC Output

- \- Can use 1 input for REC output

(REC output gain: 0, +2, +4, +6dB)

- ADC Out

- \- Built-in for ADC output (Input Att: 0/-6/-12/-18dB)

- L+R/L-R Output

- \- Built-in L+R/L-R block

- Digital Power Supply

- \- Built-in digital power supply

## FEATURES

- Electronic Volume

- \- 6 channels independent electronic volume

(0~-99dB/1dB step, -∞dB)

- Gain Control

- \- 6 channels independent gain control(0~+14dB/2dB step)

- Input Selector

- \- L/R channel 4 input selector

(Input gain: 0~+14dB/2dB step)

- Multi Channel Input

- \- 6 channels input

- Tone Control

- \- Bass: -14~+14dB (2dB step)

- \- Treble: -14~+14dB (2dB step)

## APPLICATIONS

- Receiver

- AV amplifier

- Home theater

- Mini stereo
